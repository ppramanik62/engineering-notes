# What the notes look like

I write one of these after each session, into `discussions/`. Short, a few hundred words. It's a record of the conversation, not a report card.

```markdown
# YYYY-MM-DD: <problem in a few words>

## The problem

A sentence or two, the way it was given.

## How you thought about it

The path you took, in order. Where you started, what you tried, what you changed your mind about. This is the part worth reading again later.

## What worked

Something specific you did well, and why it worked.

## Where it got stuck

Where the thinking stalled or went sideways, and what got it moving again. A question, a hint, a worked example, whatever it was.

## Where we landed

The design we ended up with, in a few lines, and what it costs.

## Worth coming back to

One thing. A loose end, an idea that didn't land, or something to read. This is what I use to pick a problem that comes at the same idea from another side later on.
```
