# Problem-solving sessions

One problem a day. We talk it through. I write a short note afterwards.

This is for getting better at looking at a problem and working out a good design for it. No code. We start wherever makes sense and go as deep as the problem needs, in plain words and rough sketches.

Started 2026-11-07.

## How a session goes

I bring a problem and describe it in plain words. You think out loud. I ask questions, push back when something doesn't hold up, nudge you when you're stuck, and tell you when you've hit on something good.

There's no script. Some days the whole session goes on working out what the problem even is. Some days you'll have a design in two minutes and we'll spend the rest poking holes in it. Both are fine, and not finishing is fine too.

Usually twenty to forty minutes. When we're done I write the note.

It's a conversation between a friend who has seen a few of these and someone learning. Saying the half-formed thing is how it works.

## Starting a session

Say "next problem" and I'll bring one.

If you've got a problem on your mind, that beats anything on a list, so lead with it. You can also just name the flavour you feel like: representation, state, time and ordering, failure, working inside a budget, or something open-ended.
